Strategy Generator: Time-of-Day, Day-of-Week & Gap Protection

Pressure-tested specification for integrating session windows (e.g. 09:00–17:00 ET), weekday filters, and pre-gap position flattening into StrategyGenome, StrategyGeneratorService, and StrategyGeneratorView.vue.

Project: Trading Bridge User: Martinfou Slug: strategy-generator-time-day-filters Zone: America/New_York (ET)

🔒 Locked Decisions (What Survived the Forge)

  • 1. Dual-Layer Architecture (Option C: Envelope + GA Regimes) Combine top-level operator-configurable Session & Flat Envelopes (exitSessionMode, entrySessionWindow, allowedDaysMask) with restricted evolvable session/weekday regimes when the GA is set to AUTO or uses time/day building blocks.
  • 2. Canonical Timezone: America/New_York (ET) All session windows, weekday masks, and pre-gap flatten cutoffs evaluate in ZoneId.of("America/New_York") so 09:00–17:00 ET automatically tracks US Daylight Saving Time and stays locked to NY RTH, the 17:00–18:00 ET CME maintenance halt, and the 17:00 ET FX rollover/triple-swap.
  • 3. Pre-Cutoff Bar Flatten Mechanic (Proposal 1) Because BacktestEngine fills market orders emitted by strategy.onBar(previousBar) at the next bar's open (bar.open()), INTRADAY_FLAT and WEEKEND_FLAT trigger closePosition(bar) and clearArmed() one bar before the break (using tfMinutes, or defaulting to a 16:00 ET flat cutoff) so exits fill before the 17:00 ET rollover spread spike and weekend gap.
  • 4. Unbroken Indicator Warmth vs. Gated Entries & Armed Stops history.add(bar) always runs on every trading bar so rolling indicators (EMA, SMA, RSI, ATR, Donchian) remain warm and never stitch across sessions. Outside the allowed entry window/weekday (or inside the 60-minute pre-flat entry freeze buffer), new entries are blocked and any pending STOP order (armedSide) is disarmed via clearArmed().
  • 5. Hybrid UI Controls + Anti-Overfit GA Guardrails (Proposal C) StrategyGeneratorView.vue provides one-click presets (24/5 Continuous, 9-to-5 Intraday Flat, NY RTH, London/NY Overlap, No Weekend Hold, GA Auto-Discover) alongside explicit Start/End Hour ET, Mon–Fri toggles, and Flat EOD / Flat Weekend selectors. When the GA mutates schedules, it is restricted to structured microstructure regimes and incurs a -1.5 complexity penalty on evolutionaryFitness.
  • 6. Higher-Timeframe Guardrail (H4 / D1) & 100% Codegen Parity Intraday hour windows and INTRADAY_FLAT apply to M5, M15, M30, and H1; H4 and D1 bypass intraday hour checks and apply only Day-of-Week filters and WEEKEND_FLAT. GenomeExecutableStrategy and StrategyGenome.toJavaSourceCode() emit identical America/New_York logic, and existing databank.json entries default gracefully when session fields are null.

🛑 What Was Rejected & Why

  • Entry-Only Time Filter (No Forced Flatten) Filtering entries to 09:00–17:00 ET leaves trades opened at 14:30 ET exposed to overnight rollover spreads and weekend gaps unless paired with an active flatten rule.
  • Flattening on the Final 16:30 ET Bar Without Lookahead Buffer In BacktestEngine, an order emitted on the 16:30 ET M30 bar fills at the next bar's open (18:00 ET or Sunday 18:00 ET), eating the exact gap it was meant to avoid.
  • Skipping history.add(bar) Outside Active Hours Dropping overnight bars corrupts ATR, EMA, and Donchian calculations at the 09:00 ET open.
  • Unconstrained 31-Bit Weekday & Arbitrary Hour GA Mutations On 1–2 year samples (minTrades = 12–15), free-form calendar bitmasks allow the GA to delete 3–4 losing trades and manufacture fake Profit Factor spikes.
  • Raw UTC Session Windows Shifts US market open and the 17:00 ET rollover/swap cutoff by 1 hour twice a year during Daylight Saving Time transitions.

⚡ Fault Lines Examined & Survived

  • Next-Bar Open Fill Timing in BacktestEngine Resolved by triggering pre-cutoff flatten using tfMinutes (or 16:00 ET cutoff) so the market close order executes on the final pre-break bar open, avoiding both the 16:00–17:59 ET spread widening and the 17:00 ET break.
  • Orphaned Armed Stop Orders (armedSide) Resolved by calling clearArmed() immediately when leaving the entry window or hitting the pre-flat cutoff so stop-breakout orders cannot fire after hours.
  • Pay-Spread-to-Flatten Churn Near Cutoff Resolved by enforcing a 60-minute pre-flat entry freeze buffer (e.g., no new entries after 15:00 ET when flattening at 16:00 ET).
  • Silent Zero-Trade Starvation on D1 / H4 Resolved by scoping intraday hour windows and INTRADAY_FLAT strictly to M5..H1 while keeping weekday and WEEKEND_FLAT rules active on higher timeframes.
